Art style: a contemporary Korean picture-book illustration of an old map, hand-painted with opaque gouache and finished with colored-pencil hatching on warm hanji paper with visible paper grain. The sea is layered muted jade green and soft teal near the coasts, deeper indigo toward the open ocean, decorated with small stylized curling wave patterns like Korean folk painting and gentle white foam lines along every coast. The land is warm ochre and sand beige with tiny painted pine trees, small rounded mountains and soft persimmon-orange accents; Korea and Japan are painted with the same care and detail as China. A few tiny simple sailing boats and a couple of soft clouds float on the open sea as decoration, never covering any coast. A decorative compass rose made only of shapes (no letters) sits in the bottom-right ocean. The paper edges are slightly darkened and aged, with a soft warm vignette. Calm, warm, inviting storybook mood. Flat top-down map view, not a 3D globe, not photorealistic, not a satellite image.
