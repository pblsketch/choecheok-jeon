Wide cinematic title illustration for a story game based on the Korean classical novel Choe Cheok-jeon (17th century). No text at all.

Scene: night on the open sea. The bow of an old wooden sailing ship points toward the right side of the picture. On the bow, in the right third of the picture, stands a slender young Korean woman disguised as a man, in plain undyed white-grey Joseon commoner mens clothing (simple jeogori jacket and baji trousers, hair tied up in a topknot under a plain white cloth headband with long tails fluttering in the wind). She holds up a small glowing paper lantern in one hand, its warm orange light falling on her face, the rope and the wooden rail; her other hand rests on the rail. She looks far ahead across the sea with a quiet, longing and determined expression. Her figure is in the vertical middle of the picture (not near the top or bottom edge).
A large full moon hangs low on the left side of the sky over the horizon, its long shimmering reflection running across the dark waves. The left half and the center of the picture are mostly open night sky and calm sea with soft mist and a few soft clouds, leaving calm empty space for a title. Far away on the horizon, a tiny silhouette of another ship with a single lantern. Deep indigo night versus the warm lantern glow.

Art style: a contemporary Korean children's and young-adult picture-book illustration, hand-painted with opaque gouache and finished with colored pencil: simplified, slightly rounded flat shapes, clearly reduced detail, visible brush marks, soft colored-pencil hatching and paper grain, a limited traditional Korean palette (deep indigo night sky, muted jade green, persimmon orange, warm ochre, soft ivory) with gentle warm glow from the moon and the lantern. The woman is drawn a little stylized with a simple gentle face that clearly shows emotion. Warm, tender, cinematic storybook mood. Not photorealistic, not anime, not a detailed digital painting. Absolutely no text, no letters, no characters, no calligraphy, no inscriptions on flags or sails, no seals, no stamps, no signatures, no captions, no watermarks, no frames or borders.
