Wide illustration of one scene from the Korean classical novel Choe Cheok-jeon (early 17th century), for a story learning game aimed at teenagers. Wide landscape composition; the main figures are clearly readable (not tiny) and stay in the central horizontal band of the picture, away from the top and bottom edges, because the picture may be cropped to a wider strip.
